## 3. What these results do and do not show

- They show the system behaves as specified on these cases, deterministically and offline, and that every recommendation is an unchanged catalog record.
- They do **not** show the system is unbiased, fair, safe, or good at recommending music. The catalog and the expectations are small, hand-made and partly synthetic, and the injection and non-music guardrails are pattern lists that a determined user can get around.
